about

My name is Lucia Morris, and I am currently a PhD student at Harvard researching AI for medicine, with a focus on vision models for 3D medical imaging. Before graduate school, I studied Computer Science at Stanford, where I completed both my B.S. and M.S. degrees. Outside of research, I love teaching!

I began working as a tutor when I was 15, and since then I have taught computer science in classrooms across the globe — from "hello, world!" to self-driving cars. I have served as a teaching assistant under some of the world's best faculty, and as a lecturer leading my own courses. You can read more about my teaching experience and how we can work together below.
